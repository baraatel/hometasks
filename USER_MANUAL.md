# HomeTasker — User Manual (Household Helpers, Maids & Service Workers)

> **Looking for the Homeowner / Admin guide?** See **[ADMIN_USER_MANUAL.md](./ADMIN_USER_MANUAL.md)** (and **[ADMIN_CREDENTIALS.md](./ADMIN_CREDENTIALS.md)** for PIN & security setup).

HomeTasker is a simple, visual daily chore checklist that works in **9 languages** and syncs live across every phone and tablet in the house. **No password or login is required** to view or complete your daily chores.

---

## 1. Pick Your Language (`🌐`)

1. Tap the **Globe button (`🌐`)** in the top corner of the screen.
2. Select your language from the list:
   - **العربية** (Arabic — layout flips Right-to-Left)
   - **English**
   - **हिन्दी** (Hindi)
   - **Filipino**
   - **नेपाली** (Nepali)
   - **සිංහල** (Sinhala)
   - **தமிழ்** (Tamil)
   - **اردو** (Urdu — layout flips Right-to-Left)
   - **বাংলা** (Bengali)
3. The app remembers your chosen language automatically every time you open it.

---

## 2. Switch Between Light Mode & Dark Mode (`☀️` / `🌙`)

- Tap the **Moon (`🌙`)** or **Sun (`☀️`)** button next to the language menu in the top header to switch between **Dark Mode** (easier on the eyes in the evening) and **Light Mode**.
- Your device remembers your theme choice automatically.

---

## 3. View Today's Chores (`Today` Tab)

- The top card shows **today's date**, **current time**, and a **progress bar** (e.g., `3 of 5 done`).
- When scheduled times are set, chores are neatly grouped into **Morning**, **Afternoon**, **Evening**, and **Anytime**.
- Each card shows:
  - A large **picture icon** (🍽️ Kitchen, 🧹 Floor, 🧺 Laundry, 🪴 Plants, 🚿 Bathroom).
  - The **chore name** and any **helpful notes** in your language.
  - The **suggested time** (if scheduled).

---

## 4. Listen to Any Chore Aloud (`🔊` Voice Readout)

- Tap the **Speaker icon (`🔊`)** on any chore card to hear the chore name and notes spoken aloud in your chosen language.
- Tapping `🔊` only reads the task—it **will not** mark the task as done.

---

## 5. Mark a Chore as Finished (`✅`)

1. Once you finish a chore, tap the **chore card** or the **round circle** on the side.
2. Your phone gives a quick vibration, a green checkmark appears, and the chore moves into the **Completed** section at the bottom.
3. **Tapped by mistake?** Tap **Completed** at the bottom of the page and tap that chore again to undo it.
4. Daily chores automatically reset fresh every morning at midnight.

---

## 6. Check the Rest of the Week (`Week` Tab)

- Tap **Week** in the bottom bar (or top bar on tablets) to see the schedule for the next **7 days** (such as Laundry days or weekly Bathroom Cleaning).

---

## 7. Kitchen Tablet Full-Screen View (`🖥️ Kiosk Mode`)

- Tap **Kiosk mode** on the top banner of the Today screen for extra-large, high-contrast cards on a kitchen or wall-mounted tablet.
- The screen stays awake automatically while Kiosk Mode is active.
- Tap **Exit kiosk** in the top corner to return to the regular view.

---

## 8. Save HomeTasker to Your Phone Home Screen

- **Android (Chrome):** Tap the menu (`⋮`) → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**.
- **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** Tap **Share (`↑`)** → **Add to Home Screen**.
